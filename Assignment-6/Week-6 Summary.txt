Assignment Summary

In this assignment, I got a basic understanding of how Apache Spark works and why it is faster than MapReduce. I used the Superstore dataset and loaded it into a Spark DataFrame to perform different operations. First, I checked the schema and understood the structure of the data. Then, I cleaned the dataset by removing duplicate records and handling missing values. After that, I filtered the data based on conditions like region and sales and selected only the required columns. I also renamed some columns, changed their data types, and created a new column for better analysis. Using aggregation and groupBy, I analyzed the data and found useful insights. I learned about lazy evaluation, shuffle, and why Parquet files are more efficient than CSV files for Spark. Finally, I created a simple data pipeline that reads, processes, filters, and saves the cleaned data. Overall, this assignment gave me hands-on experience with Spark DataFrames and helped me understand how Spark processes data efficiently.

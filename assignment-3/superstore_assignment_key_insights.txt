SUPERSTORE SALES ANALYSIS USING SQL

Objective:
Analyze Superstore sales data using SQL concepts such as Subqueries, CTEs, Window Functions, and Joins to solve business queries.

Tools Used:

* MySQL Workbench
* SQL

Tasks Performed:

* Imported Superstore dataset into MySQL.
* Created Customers, Products, and Orders tables.
* Used Subqueries to find above-average sales and highest orders.
* Used CTEs to calculate total sales per customer.
* Applied ROW_NUMBER() and RANK() window functions for customer ranking.
* Combined JOINs, CTEs, and Window Functions for sales analysis.
* Solved business queries such as top customers, low-performing customers, single-order customers, and above-average sales customers.

Key Insights:

* A small number of customers generate the highest sales.
* Some customers placed only one order, indicating retention opportunities.
* Customer sales vary across regions.
* High sales do not always result in high profit due to discounts.

Conclusion:
This analysis demonstrated the use of advanced SQL techniques to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and customer performance rankings.
